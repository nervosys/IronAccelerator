that your contribution may be relicensed by the project maintainers under
either the AGPL-3.0-or-later **or** under the commercial license offered
above. This dual-licensing model is what makes the commercial option
possible while keeping the project open.

If you are uncomfortable with this arrangement, please reach out before
contributing so we can work something out.
